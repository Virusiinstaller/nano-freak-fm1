    Every page, every knob

    What each group does, with its knobs and their ranges, exactly as the firmware has them. Open a group to read it.

    Panel and navigation

    How the FM-1's buttons map onto the synth.

    Every group of the synth is a button on the FM-1. Press it to open the group and press SELECT to step through its pages. The four knobs always edit the four controls shown on the current page. Almost every continuous knob runs 0 to 100.

    ControlFunction
    HOMEOscillator group (OSC). Also cancels a SAVE.
    ALGORITHMPicks the oscillator type (15 types).
    FX · ENV · LFO · SCL · EDIT · GLOOpen the FX, ENV, LFO, VOICE (the SCL button), MATRIX (the EDIT button) and GLOBAL groups.
    SELECTNext page of the open group.
    PRESETSThe preset list.
    OCT– / OCT+Shift the keys down or up, three octaves each way.
    ARP · SEQChoose how the keys play a pattern: the arpeggiator or the sequencer. One at a time.
    PLAYStarts and stops the arpeggiator, or the sequencer when SEQ is the mode. The screen shows ARP ON or SEQ PLAY.
    RECArms sequencer recording (it switches to SEQ). Then play the keys to record them.
    SAVEOpens the Save page. Press it again to confirm, HOME to cancel.
    Two right-most keysPitch bend down and up, always on.

    OSC · Oscillators

    Fifteen oscillator types, each with its own four knobs.

    Pick the type with ALGORITHM. The four knobs change meaning with the type (first table). Knob 4 is FOLD on most types, a wavefolder that adds harmonics, and has its own job on BASICWAVES (SYNC), HARMONIC (STRETCH), WAVETABLE (MORPH) and STRINGS (DAMP). All oscillator knobs run 0 to 100, and the screen draws a picture of the waveform.

    TypeKnob 1Knob 2Knob 3Knob 4What it is
    BASICWAVESMORPHSYMSUBSYNCBasic waves with morph, symmetry and a sub.
    SUPERWAVEWAVEDETUNEVOLUMEFOLDA stack of detuned waves.
    WAVETABLETABLEPOSITIONCHORUSMORPHA wavetable, scanned by position, with chorus.
    HARMONICCONTENTSCULPTINGCHORUSSTRETCHHarmonic content and its sculpting, with chorus.
    KARPLUSSTRBOWPOSITIONDECAYFOLDA plucked or bowed string model.
    V.ANALOGDETUNESHAPEWAVEFOLDVirtual analogue: a saw and a hard-synced square.
    WAVESHAPERWAVEAMOUNTASYMFOLDA wave bent through a waveshaper.
    TWO OP.FMRATIOAMOUNTSHAPEFOLDTwo-operator FM: ratio and amount.
    FORMANTINTERVALFORMANTSHAPEFOLDA formant oscillator.
    CHORDSCHORDINV/TRANSPWAVEFORMFOLDA chord from one key.
    MODALINHARMTIMBREDECAYFOLDA modal resonator.
    NOISEWAVETIMBRESHAPEFOLDShaped noise.
    BASSMODELSHAPESUBFOLDBass models with a sub.
    SAWMODELSLAVEBALANCEFOLDSaw models. Knobs 2 and 3 are relabelled by the model.
    STRINGSMODELSTRUCTUREBRIGHTDAMPModal strings model. Has a second page: POSITION and FOLD.

    Knob 4 in detail

    LabelTypeWhat it does
    SYNCBASICWAVES0 is off. Turning it up adds a hard-synced slave oscillator, from 1× up to 5× the pitch, restarted by the main oscillator.
    STRETCHHARMONICStretches the partials away from exact harmonics. 0 is harmonic.
    MORPHWAVETABLECrossfades toward the same position in the next table.
    DAMPSTRINGSDamping of the string or resonator.
    FOLDall other typesA triangle wavefolder before the filter. The gain rises from 1 to 7 as the knob turns up, and the effect fades in over the first tenth of the knob.

    Knob 1 models

    On these types knob 1 steps through named choices instead of a plain value. The screen shows the name.

    TypeKnob 1ChoicesNotes
    SUPERWAVEWAVESAW · SQUARE · TRI · SINEThe wave that is stacked and detuned.
    WAVETABLETABLESINSAW · SINSQR · PWM · SYNC · FORMNT · VOWELS · ORGAN · COMB · FM 1 · FM 2 · FOLD · SKEW · REZ · DIGI · PARTL · EVODDSixteen tables of sixteen waves; POSITION moves through the waves of the table.
    CHORDSCHORDOCT · 5 · SUS4 · MIN · MIN7 · MIN9 · MIN11 · 69 · MAJ9 · MAJ7 · MAJThe chord played from one key. The screen names the nearest chord.
    BASSMODELSQUARE · SAWThe bass wave. Knob 2 is its width, knob 3 the sub.
    SAWMODELSYNC · COMB · CS80SYNC: two saws, the second hard-synced to the first. COMB: a saw through a comb filter. CS80: a saw with a moving notch.
    STRINGSMODELMODAL · SYMPA · STRING · FM · QUANTThe resonator model: modal, sympathetic strings, a plucked string, FM and quantised.

    SAW: knobs 2 and 3 by model

    ModelKnob 2Knob 3What they do
    SYNCSLAVEBALANCEKnob 2 tunes the second saw up to five octaves; knob 3 crossfades the two.
    COMBCOMB TUNEFEEDBACKKnob 2 detunes the comb; knob 3 sets its feedback.
    CS80NOTCHOFFSETKnob 2 moves the notch; knob 3 offsets it.

    STRINGS, page 2

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    POSITION0–100Where on the string it is plucked or excited. The picture follows it.
    FOLD0–100Wavefold amount, as the Strings preview shows.

    ENV · Envelopes

    Amplitude envelope, cycling envelope, its mode and the amplitude mode.

    ENVELOPE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    ATTACK0–100How slowly the sound comes in.
    DECAY0–100How fast it falls to the sustain level. The release follows the same value.
    SUSTAIN0–100The level held while a key is down.
    FILT AMT0–100How far the envelope opens the filter.

    CYCLING ENV

    A three-stage envelope that can loop like an LFO, with its own graph.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    RISE0–100Time of the rising ramp.
    FALL0–100Time of the falling ramp.
    HOLD0–100In RUN and LOOP the time spent at the top; in ENV mode the level the held key keeps it at.
    AMOUNT0–100Depth sent to the matrix (cycling envelope row) and to its own destination.

    CYCLING MODE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    MODEENV · RUN · LOOPENV: a note starts it and the held key keeps it at the HOLD level. RUN: it cycles freely, rise, hold, fall, again. LOOP: as RUN, and a new note restarts it.
    DESTPITCH · OSC K2 · OSC K3 · CUTOFF · A1 · A2 · A3 · A4Where the cycling envelope goes directly.
    DEPTH0–100How much it moves that destination.

    AMP ENVELOPE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    AMP ENVGATE ONLY · ENV ONGATE ONLY: the sound is on while the key is down. ENV ON: the amplitude envelope shapes the level.

    FX · Filter, widener, reverb, delay, glide, bend, Impress and Chance

    Eight pages: the sound's filter and every effect.

    FILTER

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    CUTOFF0–100Where the filter opens.
    RESO0–100Resonance, the peak at the cutoff.
    TYPELPF · BPF · HPFLow-pass, band-pass or high-pass.
    FILT AMT0–100How far the envelope opens the filter.

    FILTER 2

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    SLOPE12 dB · 24 dBSteepness of the filter.
    DRIVE0–100 (8 steps)Saturation going into the filter.
    CLIP0–100 (8 steps)Hard clipping after it.
    MODELSVF · LADDERState-variable filter, or the Minimoog-style ladder.

    WIDENER

    A global effect that sits after the filter and before the reverb, applied to the oscillators' dry mix. It is stored with the other global settings, not with the preset.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    WIDTH0–100How wide the stereo image is. 0 bypasses the effect exactly.
    TIME1–50 msDelay of the Haas effect: a short delay on one side that makes the sound seem wider.
    SIDELEFT · RIGHTWhich channel is delayed.
    TONE0–100Darkens the delayed copy.

    REVERB

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    MIX0–100Amount of reverb.
    SIZE0–100Size of the room.
    DAMP0–100How fast the highs die away.
    PRE-DELAY0–100Gap before the reverb starts.

    DELAY

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    MIX0–100Amount of delay.
    TIME0–100Delay time.
    FEEDBACK0–100How many repeats.
    TONE0–100Brightness of the repeats.

    GLIDE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    GLIDE0–100Portamento amount.
    MODETIME · SYNC · RATEFixed time, tempo-synced, or a fixed rate per interval.

    PITCH BEND

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    RANGE0–24 semitonesRange of the two bend keys (and of MIDI pitch bend). Stored globally.
    SPEED0–100How fast the bend moves.

    IMPRESS / CHANCE

    Variation for the arpeggiator and sequencer. Chance varies the gate length of each step (a gate that comes out at nothing drops the step), a little velocity and, from 60 % Impress, the octave of a step. CH AMT and IMP AMT are live and are not saved: they are back at 100 when a preset loads.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    IMP STR0–100Impress strength: how much of the Chance variation is heard. 0 plays the pattern as written.
    CH STR0–100Chance strength: how hard each roll of the dice is. Changing it rolls again.
    CH AMT0–100How much of CH STR reaches the arpeggiator or sequencer that is playing (percent).
    IMP AMT0–100How much of IMP STR reaches the one that is playing (percent).

    LFO · Two LFOs

    Two LFOs, each with its own page and its own four assignable destinations.

    LFO 1 and LFO 2

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    SHAPESINE · TRI · SAW · SQUARE · S&H · GLIDEThe waveform. S&H is sample and hold.
    RATE0–100Speed when free, or the division when synced.
    SYNCOFF · ONFree-running, or locked to the tempo.
    MORPH0–100Blends the shape into the next one in the list.

    DEST A1–A4

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    A1 to A414 destinationsPick what each assign column controls. The same four columns appear in the matrix, so one LFO page and the matrix share them. Destinations: OSC K1, OSC K2, OSC K3, OSC K4, RESONANCE, KEY TRACK, LFO RATE, AMP, PAN, DISTORTION, CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB, VIBRATO.

    MATRIX · Modulation matrix

    Five sources against eight destinations, with an amount at every point.

    MATRIX

    Knobs 1 and 2 move the cursor, knob 3 switches the point and knob 4 sets its amount. The amount of the point under the cursor is shown below the grid.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    COLUMNPITCH · OSC K2 · OSC K3 · CUTOFF · ASSIGN 1–4The destination under the cursor.
    ROWCYC ENV · ENV · LFO · LFO 2 · KEYThe source under the cursor.
    ONOFF · ONSwitches that point.
    AMOUNT–100 to +100How much, and in which direction, the source moves the destination.

    ASSIGN

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    A1 to A414 destinationsWhat each of the four ASSIGN columns controls (the same list as on the LFO pages).

    VOICE · Voice mode

    Mono, unison, paraphonic or polyphonic.
    PageFunction
    MONOOne note at a time.
    UNISONThe voices are stacked on one note. Knob 1 is DETUNE (0–100).
    PARAPHONICSeveral notes through one filter and amplifier.
    POLYUp to 12 independent voices.

    Stepping to a page with SELECT sets the voice mode of the track.

    ARP · Arpeggiator

    Fourteen patterns, latch, swing and chord mode.

    ARPEGGIO

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    RATE40–240 BPMTempo, shared with the sequencer.
    RANGE1–4 octavesHow many octaves the pattern climbs.
    PATTERN14UP, DOWN, UP/DOWN, ORDER, RANDOM, PATTERN, AS PLAYED, CONVERGE, DIVERGE, HOLLOW, JEOPARDY, CHORD, BROKEN, ALTERNATING.
    LENGTH1–16 stepsWhere the pattern loops. With AS PLAYED it is the number of keys remembered.

    ARP TIMING

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    SWING0–100Delays every second step.
    LATCHOFF · ONKeeps the notes playing after the keys are released.

    ARP TYPE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    TYPEMONO · CHORDMONO plays one note per step. CHORD plays a chord on each step's note.
    CHORDOFF · MAJ · MIN · 7 · MIN7 · MAJ7 · SUS4 · DIM7 · POWERThe chord built on each note in CHORD type.

    SEQ · Sequencer

    Up to 64 steps, two patterns, record and step edit, Random and Mutate.

    SEQUENCE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    RATE40–240 BPMTempo.
    PATTERNA · BTwo sequences you can switch between.
    RANDOM0–99Fills the pattern with a new random sequence in the chosen scale. The number is how much.
    MUTATE0–99Changes part of the current sequence and keeps the rest.

    SEQ LENGTH

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    LENGTH1–64Number of steps.
    SCALEMIN PENT · MAJ PENT · MAJOR · MINOR · DORIAN · BLUES · CHROMATICThe scale Random and Mutate draw notes from.
    OCTAVES1–3Range of Random and Mutate.
    SWING0–100Delays every second step.

    STEP EDIT

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    STEP1–64The step being edited.
    NOTEMIDI noteIts pitch.
    LENGTH1/16 · 1/8 · 1/4 · 1/2How long the step lasts.
    TYPENOTE · RESTA sounding step or a rest.

    RANDOM

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    REC LENGTH1/16 · 1/8 · 1/4 · 1/2Step length used when recording.
    RESTS0–10How many rests Random leaves in.
    RANDOM0–99Same as on page one.
    GATE10–100 %Gate length of the steps.

    To record, press REC, then PLAY, and play the keys.

    PRESETS and SAVE

    Fifty-eight factory presets and sixteen user slots, with names.

    BROWSE

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    BANKUSER · FACTORYWhich list to browse.
    PRESETslotThe sound to load.

    SAVE

    Press SAVE, then SAVE again to confirm, or HOME to cancel.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    SAVE TO16 user slotsWhere the sound goes.
    CURSOR10 lettersWhich letter of the name you are editing.
    LETTER38 charactersA to Z, 0 to 9, space and a dash.

    GLOBAL · MIDI and startup

    Settings that belong to the instrument, not to a sound. They are stored in the FM-1's flash.

    MIDI OUT

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    ARP OUTLOCAL · BOTH · MIDIWhere the arpeggiator and sequencer notes go: the synth, both, or only out over MIDI.
    OUT CH1–16MIDI channel for notes and CCs sent out.
    OUT PORTOFF · DIN · USB · DIN+USBWhich MIDI ports send.
    KEY OUTOFF · ONSend the keys' notes.

    MIDI ROUTING

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    LOCALOFF · ONOFF: the keys do not sound the synth; they only go out over MIDI.
    CLOCK OUTOFF · ONSend MIDI clock (24 per beat), start and stop while the internal clock runs.
    THRU INDIN · USBThe port whose messages are passed on.
    THRU OUTDIN · USB · DIN+USBWhere they go. Real-time messages are not passed.

    MIDI IN

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    IN CH1–16The channel the synth listens to.
    CLOCKINTERNAL · MIDITempo from the synth, or from incoming MIDI clock.

    STARTUP

    These are saved automatically.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    POWER-ONpresetThe sound the FM-1 starts with.
    BOOT SCROFF · ONShow the start-up screen.
    START PGOSC · LASTThe page it opens on: always OSC, or the last page used.

    MIDI CC

    A change made on the panel can send its CC too.

    KnobRangeWhat it does
    About 70 CCs0–127Most panel controls answer a CC number on the MIDI IN channel (for example CC 10, 12, 13 and 14 for oscillator knobs 1 to 4, 15 for POSITION, 23 for cutoff, 83 for resonance, 7 for level, 5 for glide, 71 for bend speed, 105 and 106 for attack and decay, 64 for latch, 91 for tempo, 90 and 110 for reverb and delay mix, 2 and 3 for Impress and Chance strength). Pitch bend and program change also work.
